How the map is organized

Each layer nests inside the one above it.

  • Structure An entire building. This is what you pick from the Properties list.
  • Floor A level of that Structure. A floor may span the whole footprint, or hold only a handful of Units and Rooms — that's why picking a floor filters what's shown.
  • Unit A managed space within a floor — the area actually assigned to a Client and tracked for status, square footage, and condition.
  • Room A subdivision within a Unit — the finest-grained space on the map.
